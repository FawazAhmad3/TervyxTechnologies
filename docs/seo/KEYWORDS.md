## Research observations

Public service pages associate app-development intent with Android/iOS, cross-platform work, UX and integration scope; social marketing intent with content calendars, creative work and reporting; SEO intent with keyword, on-page and technical work. These observations inform vocabulary only; no competitor claims or package prices were copied.
